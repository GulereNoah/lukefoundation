About Us

Who we are

Luke Foundation Uganda is a non-profitable organization with the mission of improving on the living standards of orphans and vulnerable children in the community.

Read More

Vision

To meet the physical, emotional and spiritual and destitute children by providing love care guidance and highest level of education.

Our Values

Committed to integrity, inclusivity, sustainability, and love, creating accessible programs that foster respect, independence, and long-term positive impact for all.

Mission

Improving on the living standards of orphans and vulnerable children in the community.

Programs

Find out what we do

Orphan Care

A special program that provides care, love, and support to children who lost thier parents and have no one to take care of them.

Details

Malaria Camp

Malaria camp is a program that provides free malaria testing and treatment along with all other diseases screening to children and adults in our community.

Details

Hope for Education

Empowering children from less privileged and vulnerable families within our community by providing them with the essential resources they need for a successful education.

Details

Support for Disabled & Elderly Families

Assisting families who are caring for elderly individuals and those with disabilities, providing them with the essential resources they need to improve their quality of life.

Details

Volunteer Hosting

Join us in making a difference, we believe in the power of togetherness in creating a meaningful change. Our volunteering program provides an opportunity for people from all walks of life to contribute with their time, skills and passion to support our mission.

Details

Crafts Skilling

Established in our second year of operation with the aim of empowering single mothers and youth within our community by providing vocational training in craft making, pottery, and tailoring..
